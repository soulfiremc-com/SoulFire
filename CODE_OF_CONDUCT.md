# Soulfire community code of conduct

Soulfire welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.
This code applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and community discussions.
It also applies when someone represents the project in another public space.

## How we work together

- Treat people with respect and patience.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work.
- Respect different experiences and accept corrections.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Respect privacy and obtain permission before sharing another person's personal information.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexual advances, and personal attacks are unacceptable.
Do not publish private information, repeatedly disrupt discussions, or retaliate against someone who reports a concern.

## Report a concern

Contact `pistonmaster` privately on Discord through the [Soulfire community](https://soulfiremc.com/discord).
Include links or screenshots, what happened, and any context that helps explain the concern.
Do not open a public issue with sensitive details.

Maintainers review reports and limit disclosure to what they need to address the concern.
They can remove content, end a discussion, or restrict participation according to the behavior and its impact.
Maintainers also follow this code and explain moderation decisions to the affected person when practical.
